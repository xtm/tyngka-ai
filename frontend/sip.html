What is a SIP Calculator?

A SIP, or Systematic Investment Plan, allows an investor to invest a fixed amount at regular intervals. A SIP calculator estimates how those contributions could grow over a chosen period using an assumed annual return.

Tyngka separates the amount you contribute from the estimated growth so you can understand where the projected value comes from.

IMPORTANT

Things to keep in mind

  • The calculator uses the annual return assumption entered by the user.
  • Actual investment returns can vary over time.
  • The calculation is an illustration and is not a guarantee of future returns.
  • Taxes, fees, inflation, and changes in investment contributions are not included unless specifically represented by the calculator.
